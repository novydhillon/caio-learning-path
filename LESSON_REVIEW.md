# Lesson expansion review

On 5 October 2026, separate reviewers read the learner-facing lesson files for Modules 2–11 without the writers' notes or edit history. Each lesson was scored on executive decision utility (25), conceptual accuracy and depth (25), worked example and exercise (20), clarity and voice (15), and sources and jurisdictional applicability (15). A material factual error or unsupported regulatory claim capped a score at 79. Writers revised lessons below 90 and sent the resulting files back for independent re-review.

These are editorial review scores, not a certification of legal compliance or an empirical test of learning outcomes. Reviewers checked cited primary material for the regulatory and career claims they called out; a complete link audit was outside this review.
