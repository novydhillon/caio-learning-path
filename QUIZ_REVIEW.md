# Quiz quality review

The expanded course now has 114 single-answer questions across 11 module quizzes. Each question is mapped to a lesson and includes an explanation. Most lessons have three questions because they combine several executive decisions, technical controls, or quantitative concepts. The transition map, community practice, and career evidence lessons have two integrated questions each.

Three writers read the expanded lesson files and drafted separate module groups. Blind reviewers received only the learner-facing module and lesson files, including answer keys and explanations. They did not receive writer notes, conversation history, edit history, or prior review reports. Revisions below 9/10 went to fresh reviewers. The reviewers independently checked keys, arithmetic, lesson alignment, distractors, coverage, and answer cues.

The rubric assigns two points each to alignment and coverage, correctness and a single defensible answer, application and decision reasoning, and plausible fair distractors. Clarity and explanations receive one point, and pacing with one to three questions per lesson receives one point. A material key, factual, or arithmetic error caps a score at 6; a genuinely ambiguous item with two defensible answers caps it at 7.

The revisions replaced repeated example recognition with new cases and calculations, improved competing choices, broadened operational and executive coverage, and removed a predictable answer-position pattern. One portfolio allocation item had two schedules meeting the stated first-year mandate; an explicit quarterly benefit deadline now makes its key unique. All final quizzes meet the 9/10 threshold with no remaining required fixes.

These are editorial assessment-quality scores. Learner response data has not yet been collected to measure item difficulty, discrimination, or learning outcomes.
